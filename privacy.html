Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Your wardrobe

Photos of your clothes, the details you enter, your skin tone choices and your outfit history are stored only on your own device, inside this app or browser. Stylo does not upload them anywhere. If you clear your browser data or delete the app, they are gone unless you saved a backup file.

AI previews (optional)

This feature is off unless you turn it on in Profile. You choose to add a face photo and a full-body photo, and you confirm that you are 18 or older and that the photos are of you. Please do not upload photos of other people.

Only when you press Preview on me, the app sends your photos and the photos of that outfit's items to the Stylo preview service, which passes them to Google's Gemini image model to create the picture. The result is returned to you and saved on your device.

The preview service does not store your photos or the finished picture. It keeps only small daily counters to enforce usage limits: a random device code made by the app, a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address, and a count. These expire after about two days.

The service uses Google's paid Gemini API. Google's terms for the paid API state that content sent to it is not used to improve Google's products, and Google may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. Please read Google's current terms for details.

Deleting your data

In Profile, press Delete my photos and previews to remove your face and body photos and all saved previews from your device. Because Stylo's service keeps no copy of your photos, there is nothing to delete on a server. To remove your wardrobe, remove items in the Wardrobe tab or clear the app's data.

Not for children

AI previews are only for people aged 18 or older.
